## Other observations
- Room types: Entire home/apt — 25,407; Private room — 22,319; Shared room — 1,158.
- The five neighbourhoods with the most listings are Williamsburg (3,919), Bedford-Stuyvesant (3,710), Harlem (2,658), Bushwick (2,462), and Upper West Side (1,971).
- Manhattan has the highest average and median listed price in this cleaned dataset.
- Brooklyn and Manhattan together account for the largest share of listings.

## Interpretation notes
- These are descriptive statistics from a 2019 listing snapshot.
- Listing count does not prove demand, bookings, or occupancy.
- Listed price does not equal realized host revenue.
